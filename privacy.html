Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: November 5, 2025

1. Introduction

VitalCare Wellness ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and the confidentiality of your personal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or communicate with us.

This policy complies with applicable privacy laws, including the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), and applicable state and federal privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Health Information (PHI)

As a healthcare provider, we may collect and maintain the following protected health information:

  • Medical history and records
  • Treatment information and care plans
  • Insurance information and billing details
  • Contact information and emergency contacts
  • Prescription and medication records
  • Progress notes and treatment outcomes

2.2 Personal Information

We may also collect personal information such as:

  • Name, address, phone number, and email address
  • Date of birth and age
  • Preferred communication methods
  • Feedback and survey responses
  • Website usage and interaction data

2.3 Website Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information through cookies and similar technologies, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Providing medical care and treatment
  • Coordinating care with other healthcare providers
  • Billing and insurance processing
  • Scheduling appointments and reminders
  • Communicating about your care and health
  • Quality improvement and healthcare operations
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Improving our services and website experience

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With your written consent or authorization
  • For treatment, payment, and healthcare operations
  • To other healthcare providers involved in your care
  • For insurance billing and claims processing
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To public health authorities for reporting purposes
  • In emergency situations to protect your health or safety
  • With business associates under HIPAA-compliant agreements

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted transmission and storage of data
  • Access controls and authentication systems
  • Regular security audits and assessments
  • Employee training on privacy and security
  • Secure disposal of physical and electronic records
  • Incident response procedures for security breaches

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and obtain copies of your medical records
  • Request amendments to your health information
  • Request restrictions on how your information is used
  • Choose how you receive health information communications
  • File a complaint about our privacy practices
  • Request an accounting of disclosures of your health information

To exercise these rights, please contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@vitalcarewellness.com or call (555) 123-4567.

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations

You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

We provide healthcare services to minors with appropriate parental consent. We collect and maintain children's health information in accordance with applicable laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for our online services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our services after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Filing a Complaint

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with:

  • Our Privacy Officer using the contact information above
  • The Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights
  • Your state health department

We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
