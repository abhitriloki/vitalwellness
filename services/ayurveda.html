Ancient Wisdom for Modern Wellness

Ayurveda, meaning "science of life," is a 5,000-year-old holistic healing system from India. At VitalCare Wellness, we combine this ancient wisdom with modern understanding to provide personalized treatments that address your unique constitution and health needs.

Understanding Your Constitution

Learn about the three doshas and how they influence your health and well-being.

🔥

Pitta Dosha

Fire and water energy governing metabolism, digestion, and transformation. Balanced Pitta creates sharp mind and good digestion.

💨

Vata Dosha

Air and space energy controlling movement, creativity, and communication. Balanced Vata brings flexibility and enthusiasm.

🌍

Kapha Dosha

Earth and water energy governing structure, stability, and lubrication. Balanced Kapha provides strength and endurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Ayurvedic treatments and our approach.

Doshas are the three energies that govern all physiological and psychological functions. Understanding your dosha helps us create personalized treatments that address your unique constitution and restore natural balance for optimal health.

Ayurveda can complement modern medicine when properly integrated. Our practitioners work with your healthcare providers to ensure safe, coordinated care. We'll review all medications and treatments to avoid any interactions.

Results vary based on your condition and constitution. Some people notice improvements in energy and digestion within days, while chronic conditions may take weeks or months. We'll track your progress and adjust treatments as needed.

Your consultation includes detailed questioning about your health history, lifestyle, diet, and symptoms. We'll examine your pulse, tongue, and other diagnostic signs to determine your constitution and current imbalances.
