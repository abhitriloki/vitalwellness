What is Physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is a healthcare profession that uses evidence-based physical methods to restore, maintain, and promote optimal physical function and mobility. Our licensed physiotherapists employ a holistic approach, combining manual therapy, therapeutic exercises, and modern equipment to help you recover from injuries, manage chronic conditions, and prevent future problems.

Our Evidence-Based Approach

At VitalCare Wellness, we follow the latest research and best practices in physiotherapy. Our treatments are grounded in scientific evidence and customized to your specific needs, ensuring effective and lasting results.

Conditions We Treat

Our expert physiotherapists have experience treating a wide range of conditions and injuries.

Musculoskeletal Conditions

  • Lower back pain and sciatica
  • Neck pain and whiplash injuries
  • Shoulder impingement and rotator cuff injuries
  • Knee pain and meniscus tears
  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Hip and pelvic pain
  • Arthritis and joint stiffness
  • Muscle strains and ligament sprains

Sports and Activity-Related Injuries

  • Sports performance optimization
  • Pre and post-operative rehabilitation
  • ACL and knee ligament injuries

  • Achilles tendonitis
  • Concussion management

Our standard physiotherapy sessions are 60 minutes, which includes assessment, treatment, and exercise planning. Initial consultations may take up to 90 minutes for a comprehensive evaluation.

Your first visit includes a detailed health history review, physical examination, movement assessment, and discussion of your goals. We'll explain our findings and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.
