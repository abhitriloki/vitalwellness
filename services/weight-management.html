Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our weight management program and approach.

Healthy weight loss is typically 1-2 pounds per week. Individual results vary based on starting weight, adherence to the program, and personal factors. Our focus is on sustainable, long-term results rather than rapid weight loss.

No rigid diets! We create flexible meal plans based on your preferences, cultural background, and lifestyle. The goal is to develop sustainable eating patterns you can maintain long-term, not temporary restrictions.

Our program is designed to accommodate various medical conditions. We work with your healthcare providers and modify the program to ensure safety while addressing weight-related health concerns.

Physical activity is important for health and weight management, but we tailor exercises to your fitness level and preferences. We focus on finding activities you enjoy and can maintain consistently.

Maintenance is crucial! Our program includes strategies for long-term weight maintenance, including lifestyle habits, ongoing support, and periodic check-ins to help you sustain your results.
